Confesso Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-10

Confesso ("the Service") values your privacy. The Service is fully anonymous and does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your real name, email address, or phone number.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account identifier: a random anonymous ID generated automatically on first launch. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Content you create: confessions, comments, and reactions, stored together with an auto-generated anonymous nickname.
  • Settings: your chosen country, language, and theme.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, GPS location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features such as confessions and comments
  • To generate the AI "Father" comfort messages (your text is sent to an AI processor to generate a reply)
  • To keep the community safe (reporting and blocking inappropriate content)

3. Third-Party Processors

  • Supabase: data storage and anonymous authentication (server infrastructure)
  • Google (Gemini API): generates the AI comfort messages. Your text is sent solely to generate a reply and is not separately stored or used for training.

4. Retention

Content you create is kept until you delete it or request account deletion. Safety-related records (reports, blocks) may be kept as long as needed to operate the Service.

5. Your Rights

You can delete content you created within the app and request account deletion. For inquiries, contact us at the email below.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 14.

7. Content Safety

The Service provides profanity filtering, content reporting, and user blocking to maintain a healthy community.
